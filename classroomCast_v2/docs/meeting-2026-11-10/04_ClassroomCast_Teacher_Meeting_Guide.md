Prepared 9 November 2026 for the meeting on 10 November 2026. Draft - pending teacher/client confirmation.

## Meeting purpose - 10 November 2026

First formal project review before full development. Present the proposal, requirements and planned design. Obtain feedback and decisions; do not present the draft as already approved or the product as already implemented.

## Opening statement

"ClassroomCast lets a teacher share a selected screen and supported audio over a local network. Students join through a browser with a class code and receive media only after admission. Authorized administrators manage temporary network restrictions through an approved integration. Today we want to confirm the scope, infrastructure, acceptance conditions and schedule before full development."

## Before leaving for the meeting

Open all three main PDFs locally; keep this fourth PDF as your checklist. Open the existing FigJam board and check teacher viewing access. Keep the detailed Markdown/traceability files as backup. Bring a short hardware inventory if available. These replacement PDFs are drafts; the four originally named Downloads PDFs could not be inspected because the files were unavailable.

## Decisions to record

| Question | Record answer, authority and follow-up |
| --- | --- |
| Acceptance capacity | How will we test the specified 50-150 students, including 150? Agree physical-device evidence, media profile, duration and performance thresholds. |
| Deployment scope | Both campus and portable modes? School server specifications and permission? |
| Network authority | Who approves DNS/gateway changes, exceptions and emergency restoration? |
| Failure behavior | May class continue without verified restrictions (D05)? Who decides, and for which lesson type? |
| Admission and media rules | Code mode, teacher approval, student identity/block duration, browser/audio support and pause behavior? |
| Schedule / submission | Confirm three sprints and working-day calendar. What does Jan 25 cutoff cover versus Jan 27 release and Feb 3 presentation? |
| Next gate | What must change before proposal approval? Who reviews it and when? |

## After the meeting

Record feedback and decision evidence first. Update requirements, dates, risks and ownership consistently. Replace the obsolete six-sprint planning baseline only after the agreed schedule is recorded. Refine and estimate the backlog; conduct authorized feasibility tests during design. Update website status from real evidence, not from document completion.

Do not submit a final test report, completed application, final implementation documentation or approval record as completed work today. Their draft outlines can remain in the repository for later use.
