**Version:** 0.1 · **Prepared:** 9 November 2026 · **Status:** Proposed review draft; no teacher approval or product test execution claimed.

## 1. Resolve first

1. **D12:** How will we demonstrate the specified 50–150 students, including CN06 capacity for 150? What physical-device evidence, media profile, duration and quality thresholds are required?
2. **D18:** Must both campus and portable deployment be delivered? What school server/AP/router equipment, permissions and actual resources exist?
3. **D05/D15:** Who can authorize apply/restore and decide whether class continues without verified restrictions? Does exam mode differ?
4. **D02/D03:** Which code-required admission modes are supported/default? What identifies a blocked student and for how long?
5. **D20:** What work is already complete, what evidence exists, and how many hours can each member actually provide before the deadline?
