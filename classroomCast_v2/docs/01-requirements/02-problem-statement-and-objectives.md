# Problem statement and objectives

**Version:** 0.1 · **Prepared:** 9 November 2026 · **Status:** Proposed review draft; no teacher approval or product test execution claimed.

## 1. Problem and evidence boundary

Teachers need students to see detailed demonstrations clearly, join a classroom reliably and follow classroom website-access rules even when external internet is unavailable. The supplied project proposes a local platform that combines broadcasting, participation and software-managed restrictions. These are stakeholder problem hypotheses from planning, not measured research findings.

## 2. Problem-to-outcome mapping

| Problem to validate | Proposed response | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|
| Distant projected text is difficult to read | Broadcast selected screen to student laptops | Teacher/student interviews and legibility task |
| External service outages interrupt teaching | Keep application and media services local | Fresh-browser WAN-disconnected demonstration |
| Manual per-class network changes are inconvenient | Approved profiles applied by backend adapter | Operator comparison of manual setup and class trigger |
| Selected policy may not be effective | Client-path verification and truthful status | Denied/allowed/local-service probes |
| Temporary restrictions can remain after class | Recorded prior state, expiry and verified restoration | End/expiry/restart/failure drills |

## 3. Objectives

Deliver a demonstrable authorized teacher-to-student flow, preserve local service availability, prevent unauthorized classroom/media control, verify supported restrictions and restore only owned temporary changes. Measure performance against agreed conditions rather than promising capacity from a hardware specification.

## 4. Validation record to collect

Record participant role, interview date, consent/retention arrangement, current task, observed difficulty, impact, priority and confirming evidence. Teacher confirmation of the problem and expected outcomes is still required. See [measurable outcomes](../02-project-proposal/03-project-objectives-and-expected-outcomes.md).
