## First team session: 45 minutes

1. Each person confirms their role and actual weekly availability, including exams and holidays (10 minutes).
2. List available server, router, access points, Ethernet connections and test laptops (10 minutes).
3. Agree on the first sprint goal and assign its tasks (15 minutes).
4. Record the lecturer/contact who can confirm acceptance criteria and campus IT permissions (5 minutes).
5. Choose the Daily Scrum time and record decisions (5 minutes).

Begin with an end-to-end classroom demonstration and early network feasibility checks. Re-estimate after the first sprint using observed effort and working results.
